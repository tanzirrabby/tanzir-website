About Me

Engineer, researcher, leader — and occasional mountaineer.

I'm a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student at BAIUST, engineering at the crossroads of AI research and production-grade software systems.

My work spans Vision Transformers for medical and food diagnostics, real-time computer vision with MediaPipe and OpenCV, and distributed microservices architectures that scale. I build things that work in the real world — under pressure, at scale, without excuses.
